What the model assumes

Where the hours-back number comes from

The model assumes subagents prepare between 18% and 45% of the paperwork around a bid, depending on whether you let them draft the request and assemble the next action. It is a planning estimate, not a measured promise.

How the hit rate is modelled

The hit rate starts from a baseline and adjusts for how focused your bid count is, whether paperwork is prepared ahead, and how much estimating time each bid gets. Tighter pipelines score higher simply because each bid gets more attention.

Why pipeline value is not revenue

Pipeline value is bids × average job value. It shows how much work is in play, not what you will win. Hit rate and margin are separate questions.
