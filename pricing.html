Questions people actually ask

Who gets into the network?

Contractors, subcontractors and suppliers working in Southern California. A person reviews the licence, the trade and the service area before an account goes live — usually within a business day.

Do I have to use all nine modes?

No. Most shops start on Yard with Projects and Exchange; Platform switches on with Desk when a pursuit needs it. The rest switch on the same way, and they already share your job data when they do.

What do the subagents actually do?

They prepare work: scoring whether an invitation fits your trade, drafting a supplier request from the scope, assembling the next action on a pursuit. They never send, buy or commit anything. A person approves each run.

Is my pricing visible to anyone else?

Your quotes, margins and bid numbers are yours. Suppliers see the request you send them; general contractors see the bid you submit. Nothing else crosses company lines.

We already run Procore. Does this replace it?

No. Project management starts after the award. Stratagem covers the part before it — finding, pricing and winning the job — and hands the won job off with its context intact.
